# 🌟 Community, Project Showcase & Support

Welcome to the Go-Live community hub! Here is how to showcase your projects, share feedback, report bugs, or get involved.

---

## 🌟 1. Showcase Your Project
Built something awesome on your `*.go-live.me` subdomain? We'd love to feature it!

- Open a **[Community Project Showcase Issue](https://github.com/shanmukhasaireddy13/Go-Live/issues/new?template=community_showcase.yml)**.
- Share your subdomain URL, a screenshot, tech stack, and what your project does.
- Approved projects get featured in our live community activity feed!

---

## 💬 2. Share Feedback & DX Suggestions
Your thoughts help shape the future of Go-Live:
- Submit ideas via our **[Community Feedback Form](https://github.com/shanmukhasaireddy13/Go-Live/issues/new?template=feedback.yml)**.
- Share impressions on Anycast speed, dashboard navigation, or new provider requests.

---

## 🐛 3. Report a Bug
Encountered a DNS routing error or dashboard issue?
- Open a **[Bug Report](https://github.com/shanmukhasaireddy13/Go-Live/issues/new?template=bug_report.yml)**.
- Please include your subdomain name, hosting platform, and any relevant logs/screenshots.

---

## 💡 4. Request a Feature
Have a feature request or new cloud integration idea (e.g. Netlify, Cloudflare Workers, Coolify)?
- Open a **[Feature Request](https://github.com/shanmukhasaireddy13/Go-Live/issues/new?template=feature_request.yml)**.
- Vote and comment on existing ideas on the **[Enhancements Board](https://github.com/shanmukhasaireddy13/Go-Live/issues?q=is%3Aissue+is%3Aopen+label%3Aenhancement)**.

---

## 🤝 5. Contributing
Want to contribute code?
- Read our full **[Contributing Guidelines](../CONTRIBUTING.md)**.
- Apply for developer access via the **[Contributor Proposal](https://github.com/shanmukhasaireddy13/Go-Live/issues/new?template=contribute.yml)** to receive an invitation to the development repository.
